Why an underwriter can trust it

No quote, no claim.

Severity comes from the authority.NHC category · USGS PAGER · GDACS level · WHO grade. The model never generates it.
Every claim is quote-checked by code.The Verifier string-matches each quote against the fetched source. Anything that fails is dropped.
A transparent score.A 0–100 risk score with its breakdown, a confidence level, and a required "what we don't know yet" section.
